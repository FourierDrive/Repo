# Overview

This section provides a comprehensive introduction to the Power system architecture, covering its fundamental role in supplying, converting, and optimizing energy across digital embedded systems.

## Fundamentals

The power management system is responsible for distributing, regulating, and converting electrical energy to all functional blocks within an integrated circuit. 

Proper power tree design, efficient voltage conversion, and comprehensive power-saving strategies are critical for balancing performance, thermal dissipation, and battery life in embedded hardware designs.
