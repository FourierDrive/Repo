# Features

SD cards are widely used in digital equipment, storage expansion, and information exchange scenarios due to their compact convenience, large capacity, and high-speed transmission:

## Convenience

SD cards have a small physical size, making them easy to carry and store. microSD cards are particularly small and widely applicable to portable devices such as digital cameras, tablets, computers, drones, and game consoles. As long as a device supports an SD card slot (or via an adapter), most devices can use SD cards.

## Large Storage Capacity

SD cards offer various capacity options ranging from gigabytes (GB) to terabytes (TB), meeting different application requirements and capable of storing large amounts of information such as high-definition pictures, videos, and other large files.

## High-Speed Transmission

With technological advancements, the read/write speeds of SD cards continue to increase. High-speed versions such as UHS-I, UHS-II, and UHS-III support faster data transmission rates, making them suitable for applications requiring rapid writing and reading, such as 4K video recording and high-resolution photography.

## Reliability and Durability

SD cards feature a durable design that is waterproof, dustproof, shockproof, and resistant to temperature variations, making them suitable for various rigorous environments and operational under harsh conditions.

## High Scalability

Users can add or replace SD cards as needed to expand storage space with high flexibility. Compared to built-in storage, external storage methods like SD cards provide greater expandability for storage capacity.

## Security

Many SD cards support encryption technology, providing data protection functions to prevent data loss or unauthorized access.
