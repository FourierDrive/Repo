# Overview

## Background

The Secure Digital (SD) card (or SDMEM card / memory card) was jointly developed in 1999 by SanDisk, Panasonic, and Toshiba. It introduced higher storage capacities, read/write speeds, and added data encryption functions. It is widely used in digital cameras, smartphones, handheld devices, video game consoles, and various embedded systems. In 2000, the SD Association (SDA) was formed to develop and promote the card standards.

The Secure Digital Input/Output (SDIO) card was introduced in 2001 as an extension based on the SD card specification, incorporating I/O functions for devices requiring expanded capabilities, such as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth adapters, GPS modules, etc.

The physical and electrical characteristics of the SDIO interface and the SD interface are identical, devices that can support SDIO cards can generally support SD memory cards, but not vice versa.

## Features

SD cards are widely used in digital equipment, storage expansion, and information exchange scenarios due to their compact convenience, large capacity, and high-speed transmission.

* **Convenience**: SD cards have a small physical size, making them easy to carry and store. microSD cards are particularly small and widely applicable to portable devices such as digital cameras, tablets, computers, drones, and game consoles. As long as a device supports an SD card slot (or via an adapter), most devices can use SD cards.
* **Large Storage Capacity**: SD cards offer various capacity options ranging from gigabytes (GB) to terabytes (TB), meeting different application requirements and capable of storing large amounts of information such as high-definition pictures, videos, and other large files.
* **High-Speed Transmission**: With technological advancements, the read/write speeds of SD cards continue to increase. High-speed versions such as UHS-I, UHS-II, and UHS-III support faster data transmission rates, making them suitable for applications requiring rapid writing and reading, such as 4K video recording and high-resolution photography.
* **Reliability and Durability**: SD cards feature a durable design that is waterproof, dustproof, shockproof, and resistant to temperature variations, making them suitable for various rigorous environments and operational under harsh conditions.
* **High Scalability**: Users can add or replace SD cards as needed to expand storage space with high flexibility. Compared to built-in storage, external storage methods like SD cards provide greater expandability for storage capacity.
* **Security**: Many SD cards support encryption technology, providing data protection functions to prevent data loss or unauthorized access.

## Standard

The SD standard version has been released since 2000, supporting dual-voltage operation modes as well as 1-bit and 4-bit bus widths right from its initial version. Prior to version 4.00, supported SD specifications utilized a 4-bit parallel bus width, achieving a theoretical transmission speed of 104 MB/s at a clock frequency of 208 MHz.

* **Version 4.00 (2011)**: Introduced a new speed mode using low-voltage serial differential bus technology to further elevate theoretical transmission speeds.
* **Version 5.00 (2016)**: Unified the card capacity standards and speed classification specifications.
* **Version 6.00 (2017)**: Introduced a new speed mode to further enhance theoretical transmission speeds.
* **Version 7.00 (2018)**: Improved both speed and capacity, rolling out larger capacity specifications and adding support for PCIe bus transmission.
